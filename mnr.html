National Broadcasting Service

Mabruenian National Radio (MNR) is the official state broadcasting service of the Gaelic Kingdom of Mabruenia. The station provides public announcements, music programming, emergency broadcasts, cultural content, and royal addresses.

Live Broadcast

Click below to listen to the official national anthem of Mabruenia.

Current Status: Experimental Broadcast Service

Programming

  • The Royal Address – Official announcements from the Crown
  • Mabruenian Evening Music – National and international music
  • Archives Hour – Historical broadcasts and records
  • Public Information Bulletin – Government notices and alerts
  • Foreign Affairs Report – Diplomatic and international news

Broadcast Information

The station currently operates online through the national government website. Additional digital broadcasting services are planned under the Ministry of Digital Management.

Emergency Broadcasting System

MNR may be used for emergency public announcements, civil alerts, and national notifications.
