Immigration into Mabruenia

The Government of Mabruenia welcomes lawful immigration, cultural exchange, and long-term residency applications from foreign nationals. Any foreign national that resides within the state of New Mexico Has automatic right to citizenship within the kingdom when applied for.

Entry Requirements

  • Valid passport or government-issued identification
  • Declared purpose of entry
  • Agreement to Mabruenian law and customs
  • Security screening if requested

Residency Permits

Temporary and permanent residency classifications may be issued by the Ministry of Internal Affairs.

Permit Type Duration Purpose
Visitor Permit 90 Days Tourism / Cultural Visits
Resident Permit 1 Year Long-Term Residency
Citizen Applicant Status Case-by-case Naturalization Pathway

Citizenship Pathway

Foreign nationals may petition for Mabruenian citizenship through:

  • Long-term residency
  • National service
  • Cultural contribution
  • Government appointment
  • Royal decree

Restricted Imports

The following items may be prohibited or restricted:

  • Illegal narcotics
  • Unlicensed firearms
  • Hazardous biological materials
  • Counterfeit currency

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Mabruenia issue passports?

Passport and identification policies are determined by the Crown and relevant ministries.

Can foreigners apply for citizenship?

Yes. Applications are reviewed individually.

Can dual citizenship be permitted?

Dual nationality may be recognized under certain circumstances.
