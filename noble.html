Noble Houses of Mabruenia

Introduction

The Noble Houses of Mabruenia are recognized families holding hereditary or honorary noble status within the Kingdom. These houses have contributed to the development of the nation through public service, governance, diplomacy, and support of the Crown. The Royal Presidency maintains this register as the official record of noble families recognized by the Kingdom.

House of Fleming

  • Status: Royal House
  • Arms: The coat of arms of the Scottish Clan Fleming
  • Current Head: HM Queen Fleming I
  • Founded: 1306
  • Notes: The House of Fleming is the reigning Royal House of Mabruenia and serves as the official ruling dynasty. Members of the House occupy the Throne and represent the continuity of the Crown.

House of Irikladis

  • Status: Noble House
  • Arms: A coat of arms resembling Nicene seals, used for the Free Kingdom of Nicaea as well.
  • Current Head: HM Queen Consort Irikladis
  • Founded: 1800's
  • Notes: The House of Irikladis entered the Mabruenian nobility through the marriage of the Queen Consort into the Royal Family. The House maintains a distinguished position within the Kingdom's noble hierarchy.

House of Hopkinson

  • Status: Ducal House
  • Title Held: Duke (Dux)
  • Arms: The coat of arms of the House of Hopkinson, a noble family in Scotland.
  • Seat: Duchy of Hopkinson
  • Current Head: Dux Cyrus Hopkinson
  • Founded: 1400's
  • Notes: The House of Hopkinson is one of the Kingdom's principal noble houses and holds the rank of Duke. Members of the House have played a significant role in government and public administration.

House of Budzniksi

  • Status: Vicecountal House
  • Arms: The seal of Budzniski
  • Seat: Vicecounty of Budziksi
  • Current Head: Vicecount Oliwer Budziksi
  • Founded: 1500's
  • notes: The House of Budzniksi is recognized among the noble families of the Kingdom and holds the hereditary title of Viscount. Members of the house have also played a role in government and public administration.
